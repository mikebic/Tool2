Standardized 8140 Implementation Checklist

Phase 2 of 3
  • Phase 1 — Policy & Structure Established
    Brigade SOP approved; roles and responsibilities defined; unit implementation POCs identified.
  • Phase 2 — Tracking & Mentorship Operational
    Standardized roster fields, certification status, and mentorship assignment implemented in >= 75% of subordinate units.
  • Phase 3 — Proficiency Verification & Continuous Improvement
    Scenario-based proficiency checks, after-action reviews, and feedback loops integrated into routine training events.
